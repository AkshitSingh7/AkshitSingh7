### The Computer Vision Journey: From Orbit to Micro-Scale

My core expertise lies in **Advanced Computer Vision**. This passion was cemented during my time as a Research Intern at **ISRO (RRSC-West)**, where I was tasked with one of the most challenging forms of visual data analysis: remote sensing. Working with multi-spectral satellite imagery requires more than just standard CNNs; it demands an understanding of geospatial coordinate systems, band combinations, and atmospheric correction. I developed deep learning pipelines using **GDAL** and **Rasterio** to automate the mapping of sand dunes, benchmarking architectures like U-Net and Transformers to solve segmentation problems that have direct implications for national land-use planning.

Currently, I am expanding my vision capabilities into the third dimension. I am engineering unsupervised anomaly detection systems using **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**, specifically the GANomaly architecture. By applying these models to 3D volumetric CT scans, I am working to identify internal material defects that remain invisible to the human eye. This work bridges the gap between medical imaging techniques and industrial Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), proving that vision models can be as effective at detecting a microscopic crack in a turbine as they are at mapping a desert from space.

### Engineering Intelligence: LLMs and Big Data

Beyond vision, I am deeply focused on the infrastructure of modern Natural Language Processing. I recognized early on that the bottleneck for Generative AI isn't the model itself, but the data pipeline feeding it. This led me to architect **"Spark-arXiv,"** a large-scale RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system designed to act as an automated research assistant.

Instead of a simple toy project, I built this as a simulation of an enterprise-grade data lake. Utilizing **Apache Spark**, the system ingests, cleans, and chunks over 87,000 technical research papers. It leverages **FAISS** for high-performance vector search and uses a fine-tuned **Mistral-7B** model (via QLoRA) to synthesize answers. This project represents my philosophy perfectly: combining cutting-edge GenAI with the rugged, proven reliability of distributed data processing.

### The Research Edge: Quantum Machine Learning

While my professional focus is on classical Deep Learning, I maintain an active research interest in the emerging field of **Quantum Machine Learning**. I believe that the next paradigm shift in AI will come from hybrid quantum-classical networks. I experiment with **PennyLane** to design Quantum Autoencoders and QSVMs, exploring how quantum kernels can offer computational advantages in specific high-dimensional feature spaces. This work keeps me grounded in the mathematics of computation and ensures I am prepared for the post-Moore’s Law era.
